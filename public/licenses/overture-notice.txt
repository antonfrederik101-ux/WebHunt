Documentation: https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/
Attribution: https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/

This dataset is a filtered and normalized geographic extract of Overture Places,
not a complete Danish company register. Each record retains its original GERS ID,
source datasets, source entries, release, import timestamp and applicable licenses.
WebHunt changes: geographic/business filtering, normalized field names and category
labels, safe website URLs. Missing website information is not evidence of absence.
